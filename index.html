FAQ

Questions we actually
get asked

Does Recoda upload my recordings anywhere?
No. Recoda runs entirely in your browser. When you finish recording, the file is generated locally and saved to your machine. We never see your screen.
What browsers does Recoda support?
Recoda works on all modern browsers that support the MediaStream Recording API — Chrome, Edge, Brave, and Firefox. Safari support is limited.
Can I record system audio without a mic?
Yes. When choosing your screen source, check "Share system audio." You can capture system sound alone, or mix it with your microphone.
What format are recordings saved in?
By default, recordings are saved as WebM or MP4 depending on browser support. Both are highly compressed and perfect for sharing.
Is there a recording length limit?
On the Free plan, recordings are capped at 30 minutes. Pro users can record for as long as their disk space allows.
How is this different from Loom or OBS?
Loom forces you to the cloud. OBS is powerful but complex. Recoda sits in the middle: instant start like Loom, local privacy like OBS, zero setup.
